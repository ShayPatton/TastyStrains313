          Our Story         

        

          BUILT FROM LOVE. SHAPED BY RESILIENCE. CREATED TO BRING JOY.         

        

          I was born and raised in Detroit, Michigan, on Fenkell and           Monte Vista. Growing up, life was stable and full of love.           My parents were present, supportive and hardworking. My father           especially played a huge role in my life. He consistently showed           up for me and provided for our family alongside my mother.         

        

          Everything changed when my father was arrested and sentenced to           20 years in prison. His absence deeply impacted my family,           both emotionally and financially. What once felt secure became           uncertain, and my mother was left carrying the weight of our           household on her own.         

        

          Watching my family struggle during that time shaped how I view           responsibility, resilience and community.         

        

          During those years, one of my happiest memories came from my           uncle, who owned an ice cream truck. He would let me help pass           out ice cream to friends, family and neighbors. I remember the           joy on people's faces and the sense of togetherness it created.           Even in difficult times, something as simple as ice cream could           bring happiness, connection and relief.         

        

          That feeling never left me.         

        

          In 2021, inspired by those memories and my desire to give back,           I created Tasty Strains. The name represents bold flavors,           creativity and the power food has to uplift people. To me,           TastyStrains isn't just about frozen desserts — it's about           creating moments of joy, especially in the communities that           raised me.         

        

          Today, I'm taking the next step toward growing TastyStrains           into a real, sustainable business. My goal is to expand through           local retail partnerships, create opportunities and bring           something positive back to neighborhoods like the one I grew up in.         

        
                       TASTY STRAINS IS MORE THAN A DESSERT BRAND.                                   It's a story of family, resilience, community and the belief             that something meaningful can come from hardship, passion             and perseverance.
